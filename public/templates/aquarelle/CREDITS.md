# Aquarelle artwork

The flowers are by Pierre-Joseph Redouté (1759–1840): the roses come from *Les Roses* (Paris, 1817–1824), and the irises and amaryllis from *Les Liliacées* (Paris, 1802–1816). The plates are in the public domain. The scans come from Wikimedia Commons, and the *Liliacées* scans were digitally restored by rawpixel and released as CC0. Each plate was cut from its paper or balanced to white for printing onto the cards.
